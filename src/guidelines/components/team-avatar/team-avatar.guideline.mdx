Team Avatars identify a Team in Mattermost. Their rounded-square shape sets
them apart from circular User Avatars at a glance — same scanning rhythm,
different kind of subject.

<Divider />

## Anatomy

<AnatomyStage surface="sidebar-header" style={{ gap: '1rem', flexWrap: 'wrap' }}>
  <TeamAvatar initials="Ac" size="48" />

- <Num>1</Num> **Container** — the rounded square that holds the avatar
  content.
- <Num>2</Num> **Image or initials** — a Team's uploaded image, or a
  two-letter fallback on a coloured background.
- <Num>3</Num> **Badge slot** — optional Mention Badge or Unread Badge
  layered over the corner.

## Sizes

Team Avatars come in 10 sizes from 24px through 120px. The Team Sidebar uses
40px; profile and settings surfaces use 48px and up.

<AnatomyStage surface="sidebar-header" style={{ gap: '0.75rem', flexWrap: 'wrap', alignItems: 'flex-end' }}>
  <TeamAvatar initials="Ac" size="24" />
  <TeamAvatar initials="Ac" size="32" />
  <TeamAvatar initials="Ac" size="40" />
  <TeamAvatar initials="Ac" size="48" />
  <TeamAvatar initials="Ac" size="64" />
  <TeamAvatar initials="Ac" size="96" />
</AnatomyStage>

Don't render a Team Avatar smaller than **32px** — initials and uploaded
images both lose legibility below that size.

| -------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |

## Badges

Team Avatars in the Team Sidebar can show a **Mention Badge** when the Team
has unread mentions, or an **Unread Badge** dot when the Team has unread
content but no mentions. Badges are not shown above 64px or in the Active
state — once the Team is selected, the badge is no longer useful.
