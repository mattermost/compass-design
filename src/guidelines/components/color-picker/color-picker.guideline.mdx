A ColorPicker lets someone choose one color from a short, fixed palette, with
an optional hex field for a color outside it. Use it where a person labels or
themes something: a channel label color, a board option color, an accent.

Two good fits for a ColorPicker:

1. **A named palette** — a handful of colors the product has vetted for
   contrast, such as label or category colors.
2. **A palette plus a custom color** — turn on the hex field when people need
   a brand color the palette can't cover.

If the choice is one of a few labeled options rather than colors, use a
[Radio](/components/radio) group or [Select](/components/select) instead.

## Anatomy

- <Num>1</Num> **Swatch** — one palette color. Each has a name (for example
  "Blue") that screen readers announce, and it also shows as a tooltip.
- <Num>2</Num> **Selection mark** — a check on the chosen swatch, drawn in white
  or dark by WCAG contrast so it reads on any color.
- <Num>3</Num> **Hex field** — optional. Accepts `#RRGGBB` or `#RGB`.

## Behavior

- **Keyboard.** The swatches form one radio group. Tab moves into and out of
  it; Arrow keys move between swatches and select, as with any radio group.
- **Naming.** Name the group with `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby`, and give
  every palette color a `label`. Color alone never carries the meaning.
- **Custom colors.** A value outside the palette leaves every swatch
  unselected and shows in the hex field. A 6-digit hex applies as you type; a
  3-digit shorthand applies when the field loses focus. Invalid input shows a
  message and keeps what you typed.
- **Disabled.** Disable the whole group, or a single swatch with
  `disabled` on its option.
