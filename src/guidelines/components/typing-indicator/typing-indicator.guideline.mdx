A Typing Indicator tells people in a conversation that someone is composing a
message. It is quiet, lightweight, and disappears as soon as the message sends
or the person stops typing.

The component only draws the indicator. Your product decides who is typing,
when to show it, and when to hide it.

<Divider />

## Anatomy

Three dots that bounce in sequence, followed by an optional text label.

<AnatomyStage style={{ gap: '1.5rem', flexWrap: 'wrap' }}>
  <TypingIndicator label="Alice is typing" />
  <TypingIndicator />
</AnatomyStage>

Pass a `label` to name who is typing. Without one, only the dots appear and a
visually hidden "Typing" is announced to screen readers; override it with
`typingLabel`.

<Divider />

## Usage

- Place it just above the message input, outside the message list, so the
  list doesn't shift when it appears.
- Write the label as a full sentence you can translate ("Alice and Bob are
  typing"). The component does not build names or plurals for you.
- Show it for as long as typing events keep arriving, and remove it when the
  message sends.

<Divider />

## Accessibility

- It renders `role="status"`, a polite live region, so the label is announced
  when it appears.
- The dots are decorative and hidden from assistive technology.
- Under `prefers-reduced-motion`, the dots stop bouncing and stay at a steady
  opacity.
