The Call Card is an unpublished prototyping composite in
`@mattermost/compass-proto`. It is the post a call leaves in a channel or
thread: it announces that a call started, lets people join it, and becomes a
record of the call once it ends.

Reach for it whenever a call shows up in the message list. It is not the Call
Widget, which holds the controls while you are in a call.

## Anatomy

<AnatomyStage>
  <CallCard
    state="active"
    title="Call started"
    meta={`3 minutes ago by ${starterName}`}
    participants={participants.slice(0, 4)}
    onJoin={() => {}}
  />
</AnatomyStage>

- <Num>1</Num> **Call icon** — A phone in a colored disc. Green while the call
  is live, grey once it has ended.
- <Num>2</Num> **Title** — What happened, for example "Call started" or "Call
  ended".
- <Num>3</Num> **Meta line** — When and by whom, for example "3 minutes ago by"
  followed by whoever started the call. For an ended call, the duration.
- <Num>4</Num> **Participants** — A stack of avatars. The first few show and the
  rest collapse into a "+N" count.
- <Num>5</Num> **Action** — Join call, Leave, or Cancel, depending on the state.
  Omitted when the host passes no handler.

<Divider />

## States

- **Calling** — You are ringing someone in a one-to-one call. The icon pulses,
  and Cancel stops the ring.
- **Active** — A call is in progress and you are not in it. Join call enters
  it.
- **Joined** — You are in the call. Leave hangs up.
- **Ended** — The call is over. The card shows the duration and, when there is
  one, how many recordings it produced. A missed call can put a "Call back"
  button in place of the recording.

The card sits on the channel background with a thin outline and lifts slightly
on hover.

<Divider />

## Behavior

The card is presentational. The host owns the call state and passes the join,
leave, and cancel handlers; the card only decides which one to show.

Below roughly 480px of its own width the card stacks: the header on top, then a
divider with the avatars and the action beneath. It responds to its container,
not the viewport, so it also stacks in a narrow thread panel.

All visible text is a prop, including the button labels, so hosts can translate
it.
