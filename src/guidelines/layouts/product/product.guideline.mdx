The Product layout is the desktop shell with the Product Rail in place of the
Team Sidebar. The Global Header sits on top, the rail takes the leftmost
column, and the selected product fills the rest: its own sidebar (if it has
one) and its main area.

It is not specific to Channels. Any product that has a prototype can drop its
sidebar and content into the same frame, and the rail switches between them.

<Divider />

## Anatomy

- <Num>1</Num> **Global Header** — The product switcher, navigator, and global
  controls. The product name is hidden, because the rail already shows the
  active product.
- <Num>2</Num> **Product Rail** — The leftmost column. Switches products. See
  the [Product Rail](/patterns/product-rail) pattern.
- <Num>3</Num> **Product sidebar** — Optional. For Channels this is the Channel
  Sidebar; products without a sidebar leave it out and the main area takes the
  space.
- <Num>4</Num> **Main area** — The product's content, with an optional right
  column beside it.

<Divider />

## Behavior

Selecting a tile in the rail swaps the product sidebar and the main area, and
the Global Header's right-side controls follow the active product. The team is no longer
chosen from a strip in the shell, so team switching needs another entry point,
for example a control in the rail's header.

<Divider />

## Implementation

Open the Specimen tab for a live layout that switches between Channels and
several placeholder products. Compose it with `ProductShell`, passing `productRail`,
an optional `sidebar`, and the main content as children. For the Team Sidebar
version, see the [Channel](/layouts/channel) layout.
